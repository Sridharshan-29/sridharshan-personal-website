3D Printed Battery Architectures

It is my B.Tech Final Year project. This project focused on developing 3D-printed porous sodium-ion battery electrodes using Direct Ink Writing (DIW), using hard carbon for the anode and NVP for the cathode. It involves modelling and experimentally optimizing the pore architecture, followed by 3D printing and electrochemical testing to study how the electrode structure affects battery performance.

CURRENT / 02

Battery Modeling

Replicating the results of a published research paper — "Investigation of transient heat generation from high energy density Li-ion battery for effective thermal management" (Nema et al., 2025) — using PyBaMM (Python) instead of COMSOL. The paper analyses heat generation components (irreversible, reversible, ohmic, and mixing heat) in a 5Ah LGM50LT 21700 NMC battery across different C-rates (1C–5C) and temperatures (5°C–45°C) using a coupled 1D electrochemical + 3D thermal DFN model. The goal is to reproduce the paper's Fig 6 heat component plots and validate the simulation outputs against the paper's results.
